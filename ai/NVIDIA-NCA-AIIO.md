### 3. Drivers of AI Evolution

The current AI "boom" is not an overnight success but the result of three converging factors:

1.  **Explosion of Data:** Massive datasets from the internet, smartphones, and IoT devices. More data = better model predictability.
    
2.  **Computational Power Growth:** The rise of **GPUs (Graphics Processing Units)** and cloud computing allows for massive parallel processing.
    
3.  **Algorithm Breakthroughs:** Innovations in neural networks, reinforcement learning, and specifically the **Transformer model**.

### 5. Defining AI, ML, DL, and GenAI

It is helpful to view these as a hierarchy where each is a subset of the former:

-   **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The broad field of machines simulating human intelligence and decision-making.
    
-   **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI; the ability of a machine to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
    
-   **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of ML; uses **Artificial Neural Networks** inspired by the human brain to process data.
    
-   **Generative AI (GenAI):** The ability to create _new_ content (text, images, code) based on learned patterns.
    

#### The Chess Analogy

| Concept | Scenario |
|--|--|
| **AI** | A machine follows pre-defined rules to play a game of chess. |
| **ML** | A machine learns to play by analyzing thousands of past human games. |
| **DL** | A machine learns to play by playing against itself millions of times. |
| **GenAI** | A machine creates an entirely new board game based on the rules of chess. |

### 6. The Transformer Model

The foundation of modern GenAI, popularized by the paper **_"Attention is All You Need."_**

-   **Mechanism:** It uses an "Attention" mechanism to understand the relationships between words in a sequence.
    
-   **Efficiency:** Designed for parallel computing, allowing for faster training on large datasets.
    
-   **Functionality:** It acts as a **next-word predictor**. By calculating the probability of the next word (e.g., "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy... **dog**"), it can eventually generate sentences, paragraphs, and full stories.
